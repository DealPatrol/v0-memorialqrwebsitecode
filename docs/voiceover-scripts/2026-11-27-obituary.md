# Voiceover script: Obituary template and free obituary writer (text only, no audio generated)

Page: https://memorialsqr.com/obituary-template
Length target: about 40 seconds. Rendering this with ElevenLabs uses paid credits, so it needs Cole's OK first.

---

Writing an obituary is one of the hardest things a family is asked to do, usually in a hurry.

Here's a free template from MemorialsQR. Fill in the details: name, dates, the story of their life, family and service times. It drafts the full obituary and a short version for the newspaper or the program. It isn't AI, and nothing you type leaves your browser. You just edit it until it sounds like your family.

Then put the full story, photos and memories on a memorial page, and print the QR code on the funeral program. It's a one-time price with ten years of hosting.
